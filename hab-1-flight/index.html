From the HAB-1 cameras, 31 October 2026.

Our first stratospheric balloon carried the Boscon flight computer, telemetry radio and an onboard language model to 25.7 km above Solapur, and came home 62.7 km away.

Released at 12:37 IST. Burst at 25.7 km seventy minutes later. Under its parachute for fifty-four minutes. Down 62.7 km north-east of the pad, where our live predictor, re-run from every packet in flight, had said it would come down.

Flight computer

A compute device running an LLM, sealed inside the payload, at 25.7 km.

The Compute Device logged every sensor, recorded two cameras and ran the language model for the whole flight. Thin air carries little heat away, so it ran hot from the middle of the climb, and kept working.

45.2 to 86.45 °C
