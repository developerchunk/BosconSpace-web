AI in orbit. Always in contact.

Boscon Space builds AI hardware and research centres in space, and a mesh that keeps every satellite talking to the ground, every minute of every orbit.

Boscon Space AI and communication infrastructure for orbit Scroll

Compute belongs where the data is made. No satellite should wait for a pass. Boscon Space builds the hardware, the links and the ground, end to end, so that space is no limit for AI or communication.

Boscon Mesh

Every satellite can see one that can see the ground.

01 — 03
Today

A satellite reaches its ground station for minutes per pass.

Only while it is high above the horizon. The rest of the orbit, data waits on board.

With Boscon Mesh

Traffic hands off across satellites, ours or anyone's.

Whichever spacecraft has a station in view carries the signal down. Nothing waits for a pass.

Result

A live link, every minute of every orbit.

Signals reach their operators when they are produced. Ground-station elevation stops being a constraint.

Platform

Three segments. One piece of infrastructure.

01  Compute

AI nodes

Accelerators, storage and a mesh radio. Inference, training and research in orbit. Results come down, not raw data.

02  Hosting

Research centres

Larger platforms for partner, university and agency workloads, operated autonomously with a permanent link home.

03  Communication

Relay satellites

Store, route and forward. Any spacecraft equipped for the mesh is never out of contact with its ground station.

Missions

Built for work that cannot wait for the next pass.

  • 01

  • 02

    Floods, fires and storms detected from orbit, with the alert delivered while it still matters.

Progress
