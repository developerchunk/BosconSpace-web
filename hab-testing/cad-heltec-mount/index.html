Heltec stand mount

Heltec V3 receiver case on a bolted V-clamp that fits any stand within its range. All PETG, no metal: jaws, case, snap-fit lid, printed M10 bolts and thumb-nuts, press-fit lock pin. Board is Heltec's own STEP, standing on its header pins. Designed and checked in CAD; nothing printed yet.

Print (PETG)

  • Jaws print on their end face: the profile lies flat, so the V, the tongue and the bolt holes (teardrops) need no supports.
  • Bolts stand head down; thumb-nuts flat; pins head down. 4 walls, 100 % infill for bolts and nuts.
  • Case base down; the dovetail channels bridge. Lid outer face down.
  • Plate: cad/print/heltec-mount/heltec_mount_plate.3mf, centred on the K2 Plus bed.

Printed hardware (no metal)

  • 2 x M10 x 1.5 bolt, hex head captive in the jaw: 55 mm for pipes up to 29 mm (this stand), 75 mm up to 46.6 mm.
  • 2 x knurled thumb-nut, 28 mm across, tightened by hand.
  • Lid snaps on with 4 hooks; press the barbs in through the side windows to open it.
  • Lock pin: tap it into the tongue through the case floor (0.15 mm press fit).
  • Bought parts left: u.FL to SMA-jack bulkhead pigtail; N(M)-SMA(M) adapter to the Yagi.
