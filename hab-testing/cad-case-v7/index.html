How it goes together

  1. Walls A and B meet: their fingers interleave.
  2. Vertical key slides down through all three fingers.
  3. Lid comes down: its fingers interleave with the walls' top fingers.
  4. Lid key A pushed fully in from the corner face.
  5. Lid key B slides in and crosses just in front of key A's end, trapping it.
  6. Each key gets its clip: pushed into the slot above the key, through the round hole in the key, turned 90° until it clicks. Turned, its crossbar sits under the slot's edges and cannot come out.

Numbers (CAD)

  • Edge block 14 × 14 mm solid; key 6 × 6 mm; 4 mm solid round each key.
  • Fingers 27 mm; lid owns the finger next to its corner block.
  • Panel: 1.2 mm skin + 8 mm hex cells, 18 mm across, 1.6 mm walls, open inward.
  • Clip: 11 × 7 × 3.3 mm, Ø3 mm stem through a Ø3.3 mm hole in the key, crossbar under 1.2 mm ledges, click 0.39 mm (turning only).
  • Test piece mass: 166 g PETG.
