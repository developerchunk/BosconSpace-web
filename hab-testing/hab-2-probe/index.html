How the heat leaves

  1. SoC to spreader: Carbice-class pad, then an 80 × 80 × 3 mm copper spreader on the module.
  2. Paraffin buffer: an aluminium can of 0.2 kg paraffin on the spreader. Its grooved base clamps the pipes, and it soaks up heat on the hot pad.
  3. Eight heat pipes, two per panel, pinwheel: 6 mm copper, sintered wick, methanol (water freezes in the cold condenser; sizing in docs/08). Each leaves the spreader sideways, crosses 50 mm of foam in its own bore and bends up. With one pipe failed, the other seven still carry 25 W (computed, 1.39x after model error).
  4. Condenser above evaporator: the vertical leg sits in an aluminium saddle on a panel, 16.5 mm or more above the evaporator, so gravity returns the liquid. Heat flows up, out and not back: the thermal diode of docs/05.
  5. White panels: 4 × 150 cm² aluminium, ZnO white outside (absorbs 16% of sunlight, emits 93% of IR). Bare aluminium faces the foam. Fins stand vertical in the climb airflow.
  6. Sun out: aluminised Mylar on the lid (low emissivity) with windows over the two GPS patches. No vents.

Numbers

  • Outer box 260 × 260 × 280 mm; cavity 160 × 160 × 180; walls 50 mm (CAD).
  • Mass , against the 3,000 g single-package "heavy" line (docs/24). Most masses are assumed; click parts for their source.
  • Radiators 600 cm² in all: junction 86 °C at 25 W, 30 km, if the die-to-panel path is 1.8 K/W or less (computed, docs/05; not measured).
  • Pack 4S2P Molicel P45B, 8 cells, 575 g: 96 Wh at 0 °C against 68 / 121 Wh nominal / upper (docs/06).
  • Checks: no part overlaps another, all inside the cavity, condenser above evaporator. hab-2/cad/check_probe.py; broken once on purpose (pack dropped 15 mm) and caught.
