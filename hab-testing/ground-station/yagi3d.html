Mount parts (proposal)

  • Tilt axle: 20 NB GI pipe (26.9 mm OD), 400 mm, horizontal, through a tee at the mast top. The antenna's own plate clamps to it with its two U-bolts. Turning the axle tilts the boom; elements stay vertical. Lock with a bolt through the tee.
  • Antenna 250 mm along the axle from the mast, so the vertical metal mast is not next to the vertical elements, and the boom can tilt to 90 deg without hitting it.
  • Pan: the whole mast turns in the tripod collar. Azimuth ring on the collar (fixed, 10 deg marks, zero to true north), pointer and handle on the mast at 1.2 m.
  • Guys: 3 at 120 deg on a loose guy ring at 2/3 of the height, so the mast can turn inside it. Needed above 2.5 m.
  • Radio: Heltec in a printed case on a universal V-clamp, hanging under the axle 125 mm from the mast (cad/heltec_mount). It tilts with the antenna, so the coax never bends. OLED faces down to the operator. SMA bulkhead toward the antenna, N(M)-SMA(M) adapter onto the antenna's white coax; USB end toward the mast.
  • Clamp range: round pipe 13 to 46.6 mm, square tube 10 to 40 mm, 2 printed M10 bolts + thumb-nuts (55 mm on this axle, 75 mm for the full range); all PETG, no metal.
  • Balance: the antenna's centre of mass sits roughly 0.3 m in front of the axle (estimate): about 1.2 N m at 400 g. Tighten the tee bolt, or add about 0.4 kg on a 0.3 m rod behind the axle.
