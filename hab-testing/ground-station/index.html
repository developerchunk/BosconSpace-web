How to mount the Yagi

How to mount the payload downlink antenna

  • 3 dBi duck on an SMA bulkhead in the centre of the bottom face, hanging straight down, in line with the suspension line. That keeps it vertical, so it matches the vertically polarised Yagi.
  • Heltec inside, close to the bulkhead, 15 cm u.FL pigtail. Tape the u.FL down.
  • Both GPS patches on the top face, under the lid, facing up: about 0.30 m from the duck (the 30 cm box). Check before flight that the GPS signal strength (C/N0) does not drop while the beacon transmits.
  • A vertical duck has a null straight down. The ground station only sees that null when the balloon is nearly overhead, where the range is short; the margin chart includes it.
  • Keep the duck out of both cameras' view, and away from the Pi and the power bank, which are metal and noisy.
