How it prints

  1. Level 1 and 2: the lid and floor (same part), outer face down.
  2. Levels 3 to 6: the 4 walls, turned so their cell walls sit exactly on the caps' cell walls.
  3. Between every two panels, a 1.0 mm gap: 0.2 mm air, a 0.6 mm red release layer of thin lines that only sits on cell walls and edge blocks, 0.2 mm air. The same spacing as the slicer's own support interface.
  4. Grey towers from the bed hold up the wall fingers that hang over the caps' finger gaps.

Numbers

  • Creality Print: 1 d 13 h 36 min, 1270 g PETG, one plate.
  • The same box as 7 separate plates: 34 h 42 min in total (2.9 h less printer time, but 6 plate changes).
  • Modelled support: 56 g (CAD).
